# VeloxaCare — The One-Page Pitch (master this)

**Goal:** in ~4 minutes, get to "yes, let's run the pilot." Talk less, demo more.

---

## 1. THE HOOK (10 seconds)
> "When a patient walks out of your clinic, you lose sight of them — and more than
> half stop taking their medication within weeks. VeloxaCare is the nurse that
> follows every patient home, on WhatsApp, automatically."

## 2. THE PROBLEM (20 seconds — make them nod)
> "Here's what you already know: patients feel better, so they stop their drugs.
> They can't afford the next refill and say nothing. Their blood pressure climbs
> silently between visits. Your nurses don't have time to call everyone. And you
> only find out at the next appointment — if they even show up."

*In Ghana, 55% of chronic patients don't take their medication as prescribed —
and 96% of them say the reason is cost, not forgetting.*

## 3. THE SOLUTION (20 seconds)
> "VeloxaCare checks in with every patient on WhatsApp — no app to install. It
> reminds them daily, asks for their blood pressure weekly, and — this is the part
> nobody else does — it understands *why* a patient is slipping. Forgot? Cost?
> Side effect? Each one triggers the right action, and your team sees it instantly."

## 4. THE DEMO (90 seconds — this is the whole pitch) → *open the dashboard*
1. **"This is your clinic, two weeks in."** — 6 patients, sorted by urgency, alerts already up.
2. **Click Kofi (red).** "He can't afford his drugs — flagged 9 days ago." Tap **Cost 💸**. → bot routes him to an NHIS-covered alternative. *"You don't lose the patient. The pharmacy doesn't lose the sale."*
3. **Click Kwame.** Tap **High BP 🔴** → red alert appears live. *"Your nurse knows this second — not in three weeks."*
4. **Click Weekly Report.** *"Every patient, ranked by risk, on the doctor's desk before clinic. Zero work for your staff."*
5. **Enroll them live** (their name + number). *"That's the entire workload — 30 seconds after a consultation."*

## 5. THE PROOF (15 seconds)
> "This isn't theory. WhatsApp follow-up moved adherence from 58% to 68% in a
> trial of diabetes and hypertension patients. Daily check-ins raise the odds of
> a patient staying in care by 20%."

## 6. THE BUSINESS CASE (20 seconds — say this to whoever pays)
> **To a clinic:** "Patients who stay in care come back. This is retention you can measure."
> **To a pharmacy:** "A chronic patient is worth ~12 refills a year. Half drop off
> after three. We keep them refilling — and when they can't afford it, we switch
> them to your generic instead of losing the sale. You pay us per active patient;
> we make you more than we cost."

## 7. THE OFFER (15 seconds — the close they can't refuse)
> "Here's what I propose: a **free 60-day pilot**. One condition — hypertension.
> 30 of your patients. **We do the work; you just get the weekly report.** No
> setup cost, nothing for your staff to learn. At day 60 I show you retention
> versus your normal drop-off. If it didn't help, you owe nothing."

## 8. THE ASK (5 seconds — then stop talking)
> "Can we pick 30 patients and start Monday?"

---

### If they push back
- **"Is it safe / will it give medical advice?"** → "It never diagnoses. Danger signs
  like high BP or chest pain are flagged to your nurse by fixed clinical rules — a
  human always decides. The AI only handles reminders, language, and summaries."
- **"Our patients aren't tech-savvy."** → "If they can receive a WhatsApp message,
  they can use it. They just reply YES. No app, no data plan needed."
- **"We have no budget."** → "That's why the pilot is free. Prove it first, decide later."
- **"Data privacy?"** → "Their data stays protected and is only visible to your care
  team, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act."

### Remember
One clinic. One condition. One proof. **The demo sells it — let it.**
