## FIELD: "What are you offering?"

VeloxaCare — an AI-powered patient follow-up assistant on WhatsApp for clinics and
hospitals in Ghana. After a patient leaves the clinic, VeloxaCare automatically follows
up: reminding them about their medication and treatment steps, checking in on their
condition or recovery, detecting when something is wrong (and WHY — forgot, cost, side
effect), and alerting the clinic's care team early. No app needed — patients just use
WhatsApp. Clinic staff get a live dashboard plus a one-page summary of every patient
before each visit, with no extra work for nurses. We are offering selected clinics a
FREE 60-day pilot, and a sample of the dashboard is attached to this email.

>>> IMPORTANT — TAILOR THE EMAIL TO THE CLINIC'S SPECIALTY <<<
Read the recipient clinic's name/type and make ALL examples specific to what THEY treat.
Never mention conditions they don't handle (e.g. do NOT mention blood pressure or
diabetes to an eye clinic). Use the right angle:

- Eye clinic / ophthalmology → glaucoma eye-drop adherence, post-cataract-surgery
  follow-up, diabetic-retinopathy screening reminders, detecting missed eye drops.
- General / family clinic / polyclinic → hypertension & diabetes medication adherence,
  chronic-care follow-up between visits.
- Dental clinic → post-procedure care instructions, 6-month recall reminders,
  recovering missed appointments.
- Maternity / OB-GYN → antenatal visit reminders, pregnancy danger-sign check-ins,
  postnatal and newborn follow-up.
- Cardiology / heart clinic → blood-pressure and heart-medication adherence, symptom
  monitoring between visits.
- Dermatology / skin clinic → treatment-course adherence, follow-up check-ins.
- Pediatric / children's clinic → vaccination-schedule reminders, follow-up after illness.
- Orthopedic / physiotherapy → post-op rehabilitation and exercise adherence check-ins.
- ENT, fertility, oncology, renal, etc. → adapt the same way to that specialty's
  follow-up and treatment-adherence needs.
- If the specialty is unclear → stay general: "keeping your patients on track with
  their treatment and follow-up after they leave your clinic."

ALWAYS: open by referencing the clinic by name; lead with a pain point relevant to THEIR
specialty; keep it under 150 words; warm, respectful, professional; simple clear English
suitable for Ghana; mention the FREE 60-day pilot; mention the attached dashboard sample;
end with a low-pressure call to action. Do not over-claim or use hype. Sign off as
"John Evans Okyere, VeloxaCare".

---

## FIELD: "What should the email try to achieve?"

Get the clinic interested enough to reply and agree to a short, no-pressure meeting (in
person or a quick call) where John gives a live demonstration of VeloxaCare and explains
the free 60-day pilot. The email should build credibility, show John understands their
specific type of clinic, and make saying "yes to a demo" feel easy and risk-free.

---

## FIELD: "Call to action"

Ask if they're open to a quick 15–20 minute demo this week — in person or by call.
Make it clear there is NO commitment and NO cost: the pilot is completely free, and all
they need to do right now is show interest. They are not signing up for anything by
agreeing to the demo.

---

## FIELD: "Tone"

Professional (warm, respectful, confident — not salesy or pushy).
