### Long-form source answer — do not paste into the form

Across Ghana, healthcare is often fragmented between patients, clinics,
hospitals, pharmacies, laboratories and insurers. Patients repeat their medical
history, wait through inefficient processes, lose track of referrals and
follow-ups, and may fail to receive medicines because of cost or availability.
Healthcare workers spend valuable time on paperwork, claims, documentation and
coordination instead of direct patient care. Facility managers also lack a live
view of patients, staffing, stock, revenue and outcomes.

VeloxaCare aims to solve this by building an AI-native operating system for
healthcare facilities. It will manage the complete patient journey, from
registration and triage to consultation, laboratory services, pharmacy, billing,
NHIS claims, referrals and follow-up.

AI agents will receive patients through web, WhatsApp, SMS, USSD and voice;
convert natural conversations into structured records; assist clinicians with
documentation; identify urgent cases using approved protocols; track referrals
and appointments; predict medicine stockouts; prepare claims; and continuously
follow up with patients.

VeloxaCare will remind patients to take their prescribed medicines, attend
check-ups, complete tests and request refills. Patients can confirm completion,
report side effects or explain barriers such as cost. The system records these
responses and alerts the appropriate healthcare worker when intervention is
needed.

The system will prioritise the right tasks for nurses, doctors, pharmacists and
managers. AI will not independently diagnose or prescribe. High-risk decisions
will remain under licensed healthcare professionals, with every AI action
explainable and auditable.

VeloxaCare will build the healthcare operating system around AI from the
beginning, making care more coordinated, accessible, affordable and efficient
for Ghanaian patients and providers.

## Core reminders to preserve in future answers

Medication and check-up reminders are central to VeloxaCare. The system should
remind patients to:

- Take prescribed medicines at the appropriate time
- Attend scheduled check-ups
- Complete laboratory tests
- Refill medicines before they run out
- Confirm completion or report a problem

Repeated missed reminders should trigger a risk review and human follow-up.
