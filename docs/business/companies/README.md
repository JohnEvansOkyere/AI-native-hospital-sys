### How to use this folder
1. New lead → make a `companies/<name>/` folder and add a row to the table above.
2. Pull their **field, location, contact** from their website before writing anything.
3. Pick **one brand** and stick to it for that company.
4. Match the **stage** to the asset: cold email → reply email → discovery (meeting) → proposal PDF → free 60-day pilot.
5. Keep **Status** and **Next step** current.

> Product / pitch material that is not company-specific stays at the repo root:
> `LEAD-GEN-BRIEF.md`, `PITCH.md`, `DISCOVERY-CALL.md`, `DEMO.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
