## 2. The leak this points to

That ticket was not a one-off bug. It is a symptom of where you are most exposed:

- **Your front door is slow and dumb.** Prospects and members who reach out get routed,
  queued, ticketed, or answered seven hours later, instead of understood in the moment.
  Some of them, like me, were ready to do business at midnight. How many simply went quiet
  by morning?
- **Your members underuse the very benefit you sold them.** You unlock up to GHS 4,000 in
  care value per employee. But care value only becomes loyalty when a member *uses* it. A
  member who forgets they have it, doesn't know their balance, can't find the nearest
  clinic, or never activates their card, is a member who felt nothing this year.
- **A member who felt nothing is a renewal you lose.** Companies renew Rivia when their
  people *felt the value.* Silent, unengaged members are exactly the ones who make an HR
  manager question the spend when the invoice comes around.
- **Your virtual-care and support staff spend their day on repetitive questions** that an
  intelligent agent could answer instantly, so your clinicians can focus on actual care.

## 3. What we propose: a Rivia-branded WhatsApp concierge

WhatsApp is where Ghanaians already are. Ananse AI Labs builds you an **intelligent
WhatsApp agent, branded as Rivia**, that becomes the smart front door you are missing. It:

1. **Understands intent, not tickets.** A prospect is routed to your sales team, a member
   issue to care, a real emergency told to call emergency services. The right path, every
   time, the opposite of what I experienced.
2. **Onboards every new member.** Walks them through activating their card and getting
   started, in minutes, in plain language, the moment they join.
3. **Answers the questions your members actually ask, 24/7.** *"What's my care balance?"
   "Which clinic is nearest and what are its hours?" "Is this covered?" "How do I book a
   visit?" "How do I reach a doctor?"* Instantly, on your behalf.
4. **Drives members to use their care.** Gentle, branded nudges that get members into your
   clinics and onto your free virtual care, turning unused care value into real visits.
5. **Routes to your free virtual care and triages politely.** It never diagnoses; it
   guides members to the right Rivia service and escalates anything clinical to a human.
6. **Gives your team a clear view** of what members are asking and where engagement is
   slipping, so you can act on it.

### The safety boundary we will not cross

The agent **never diagnoses** and never replaces your clinicians. Anything clinical or
urgent is escalated to your team or, in an emergency, to local emergency services, exactly
as your own policy states. All data is handled in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act,
2012 (Act 843), consistent with your existing registration with the Data Protection
Commission.
