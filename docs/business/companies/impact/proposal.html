2 · How it works: your Impact-branded WhatsApp assistant

WhatsApp is already how your patients reach you. Ananse AI Labs makes it intelligent, an assistant branded as Impact that stays with every patient after they leave. It:

  1. Books appointments 24/7, day or night, weekend or holiday, the moment a patient thinks of it, without tying up your front desk.
  2. Reminds patients of their appointments so far fewer no-shows leave your specialist and diagnostic slots empty.
  3. Recalls patients for what they are due, the antenatal scan, the physio session, the 6-month dental check, the chronic-care follow-up, the annual wellness screening, on time, every time.
  4. Answers the questions patients actually ask, "What are your hours?" "Do you take my insurance?" "Do you do EEG / 4D scans / root canal?" "Where are you located?", instantly, on your behalf.
  5. Follows up after a visit or procedure, checks recovery, reminds about medication and next steps, and quietly flags anyone who needs your team's attention.
  6. Reminds patients of everything else you offer, so a single-service patient discovers the full range of Impact, all under one roof.

A safety boundary we will not cross: the assistant never diagnoses and never replaces your clinicians. Anything clinical or urgent is escalated to your team, and in an emergency the patient is told to seek emergency care. The AI only handles booking, reminders, recalls, answers, and follow-up. Patient data is handled in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
