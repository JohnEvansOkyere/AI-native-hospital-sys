# LinkedIn post, Ananse AI Labs (keep it light, don't over-reveal)

Most clinics lose the patient the moment they walk out the door.

Not because the patient stopped caring. Because nobody was there in the long, quiet gap
between visits, the weeks and months where people forget, drift, and don't come back.

A reminder app doesn't fix this. A reminder nobody checks is just a notification.

So we've been building something quieter and smarter: a WhatsApp assistant that stays with
a patient after they leave, in the clinic's own name. It doesn't just remind, it follows
up, it knows whether the advice was actually followed, and it gently brings people back
when they're due.

No app to download. No extra work for the staff. Just the clinic, in the patient's pocket.

I sat with a clinic this week that felt this problem in their bones, and watching it click
for them is the whole reason I do this.
