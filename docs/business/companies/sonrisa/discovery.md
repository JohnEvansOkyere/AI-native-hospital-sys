# Sonrisa Dental, Discovery Meeting Guide
### The 7 questions (Brian Tracy: Examination, Diagnosis, Prescription)

> A doctor never prescribes before examining. In this meeting I do not pitch. I ask, I
> listen, I take notes. I stay in the questions until they have *felt* the pain. The demo
> is the prescription, I earn it only after the diagnosis.

**Three rules (do not break them):**
1. **Ask, then go quiet.** Whoever asks the questions controls the room. Let silences sit.
2. **Take notes visibly.** It signals their answers matter, and it feeds the pilot pitch.
3. **Do not demo early.** Stay in Examination and Diagnosis until Q5 and Q6 land. The demo
   hits ten times harder after the pain is real.
