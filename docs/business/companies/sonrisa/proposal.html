1 · What we saw together

When we met, one thing was clear: Sonrisa already cares about what happens to a patient after they leave. You give real aftercare advice. You want them gargling morning and evening after an extraction. You want them back in six months so you can check that the work held and the advice was followed.

But here is the honest gap, in your own words:

  • You send reminders to gargle, but you have no way of knowing if the patient actually did it. A reminder goes out into silence. Did they obey? You only find out at the next visit, if there is a problem, or never.
  • Dental care is not like pressure or diabetes, where a patient takes a drug every single day for life. Your follow-ups are spread out, sometimes months, sometimes years apart. And in that long silence between visits, patients drift. They forget. They assume they're fine. They don't rebook.
  • The patients who heal badly, or who ignored the aftercare, are exactly the ones you don't hear from, until they walk in with a complication, or worse, post about it online.

That long, silent gap is where Sonrisa loses patients, and loses the recall revenue that should have been yours.

2 · What VeloxaCare really is, Sonrisa in your patient's pocket

VeloxaCare is not just a reminder app. A reminder that nobody checks is only a notification. VeloxaCare reminds, then asks, then knows whether the patient actually did it, and tells you who didn't.

It is your clinic's own assistant that goes home with every patient, on WhatsApp, under your name, so they never feel Sonrisa let go of them. After a procedure, your patient keeps hearing from Sonrisa:

  1. It guides the aftercare, day by day. After an extraction, it walks them through the gargling, morning and evening, the do's and don'ts, what is normal and what is not, in simple language, on your behalf.
  2. It confirms they actually did it. Instead of guessing, it checks in: did you gargle today? When a patient says no, or goes quiet, your team sees it. You finally know who is following advice and who isn't, while you can still do something about it.
  3. It catches a recovery going wrong, early. If a patient's answers suggest pain, swelling, or trouble, it flags them to your team so you can call them before it becomes a complication, or a bad review.
  4. It carries the patient across the long gap and brings them back. Six months, a year, whatever the recall, it keeps gentle, branded contact alive in that silence and brings them back for their check-up, so your recall slots actually fill.
  5. It answers their questions, any time. Hours, location, "is this normal after my extraction?", "when do I come back?", "do you do whitening?", answered instantly, day or night, as Sonrisa.
  6. It hands your team a simple summary, who followed their aftercare, who is due back, who is at risk, with no extra work for your staff.

The safety boundary we will not cross: VeloxaCare never diagnoses and never replaces your judgement. The guidance it gives is the general aftercare you approve, and any danger sign is flagged to your team by fixed rules, a human always decides. Patient data stays visible only to your team, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
