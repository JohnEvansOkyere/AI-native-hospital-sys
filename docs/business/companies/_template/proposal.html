1 · The real cost to your clinic

You did the hard work. The consultation. The diagnosis. The prescription. You earned that patient's trust.

Then they walk out the door, and you never hear from them again.

That is not just a health problem. That is your revenue walking out the door:

  • A patient who doesn't come back is a follow-up visit you don't bill, drugs you don't dispense, tests you don't run, income you already earned the right to, gone.
  • When that patient next needs care, they may go to another clinic, a pharmacy, or a chemist down the road. You did the work; someone else keeps the patient.
  • The patients who quietly stop, forgot, couldn't afford it, got confused about the instructions, say nothing. You only find out when it's too late, or never.
  • And the patients you do keep rarely tell their family about you, because the moment they left, they stopped hearing from you.

In most clinics, half or more of patients who should return for follow-up never do. Picture your own appointment book: half of it could be full, and isn't. That is the leak. Every empty follow-up slot is money you already worked for, sitting unclaimed.

2 · What VeloxaCare really is, your clinic, in your patient's pocket

VeloxaCare is not a reminder app. It is your clinic's own assistant that goes home with every patient, on WhatsApp, under your name, so they never feel you let go of them. After every visit, your patient keeps hearing from your clinic:

  1. It reminds them to take their medication, complete their treatment, and come back for their next appointment, so your follow-up slots actually fill.
  2. It answers their questions, any time. The things they were too rushed or too shy to ask in the consulting room, "Can I take this with food?" "Is this side effect normal?" "When should I come back?", answered instantly, in simple language, on your behalf.
  3. It gives guidance for their specific condition, diet, lifestyle, the do's and don'ts for what they are being treated for, the advice your doctor would give if there were time for every patient. All within safe limits you approve.
  4. It notices when a patient is slipping, and why (forgot, cost, side effect, ran out, confused), and quietly alerts your team so you can act before you lose them.
  5. It hands your team a one-page summary of every patient before each visit, who is on track, who is at risk, and why, with no extra work for your staff.

The effect: your patient never feels abandoned. Your clinic stays in their pocket, in their messages, in their mind. When they or their family next need care, you are who they think of, not the clinic down the street.

A safety boundary we will not cross: VeloxaCare never diagnoses and never replaces your judgement. The guidance it gives is general health education within limits you set, and any danger sign is flagged to your staff by fixed rules, a human always decides. Patient data is visible only to your care team, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act.

3 · What this does for your clinic, in money

This is the part that matters: VeloxaCare is a retention engine. It turns one-time visits into ongoing patients, and ongoing patients into revenue and referrals.

  • More returning patients, your follow-up slots fill instead of sitting empty.
  • More repeat revenue, every returning patient is another consultation, prescription, test. Patients who stay in care are worth several visits a year, not one.
  • More referrals, a patient who feels cared for after they leave tells their family and friends. Your best marketing is a patient who never felt abandoned.
  • A clinic that stands out, very few clinics in Ghana follow up with patients at all. Doing it makes you the clinic people trust and come back to.
  • Less waste, fewer empty slots, fewer no-shows, less nurse time spent chasing people by phone.
Do the math with your own numbers (illustration)

Say your clinic sees 150 follow-up patients a month, and about half don't return, that's ~75 patients drifting away every month.

If VeloxaCare brings back just 20 of them, and an average return visit (consultation + drugs + tests) is worth GHS 200:

20 × GHS 200 = GHS 4,000 / month → ~GHS 48,000 a year

And that's before referrals, repeat visits, and the patients you keep for years instead of losing after one. Plug in your real patient count and visit value, the number gets big fast.
