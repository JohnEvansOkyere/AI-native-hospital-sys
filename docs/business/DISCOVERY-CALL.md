**Keeping your patients on treatment after they leave your clinic.**

Dear Doctor,

More than half of patients with conditions like hypertension and diabetes stop taking
their medication within weeks of leaving the clinic — often because they forget, or
quietly because they cannot afford the next refill. By the time they return, the
condition has frequently worsened.

**VeloxaCare** is a simple WhatsApp-based assistant that follows up with your patients
automatically. It reminds them daily to take their medication, checks their blood
pressure weekly, and — most importantly — understands *why* a patient is slipping
(forgot, cost, side effect) and alerts your care team so you can act early. There is
no app to install; patients simply reply on WhatsApp.

Before each consultation, your doctors receive a one-page summary of every patient —
who is on track, who is at risk, and why — with no extra work for your nurses.

I would like to offer your clinic a **free 60-day pilot** with 30 hypertension
patients. We do the work; you simply receive the weekly report. At the end, we show
you the difference in patient retention. If it doesn't help, there is no cost and no
obligation.

**Could we meet for 20 minutes so I can show you a live demonstration?**

## 🧭 The three rules (don't break them)
1. **Ask, then shut up.** Whoever asks the questions is in control; whoever listens, wins.
2. **Take notes visibly.** It signals their answers matter — and feeds the weekly-report pitch.
3. **Don't demo early.** Stay in Examination until Q3–Q5 land. The demo hits 10× harder after the diagnosis.
