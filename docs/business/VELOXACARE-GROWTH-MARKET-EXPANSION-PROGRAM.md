# VeloxaCare Growth & Market Expansion Program

## Project period

**27 July 2026 to 31 August 2026**

## Company structure

- **Parent company:** Veloxa Technology Limited
- **Product/business unit:** VeloxaCare
- **Program name:** VeloxaCare Growth & Market Expansion Program
- **Initial market:** Private clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies in Ghana

## 1. Why I am starting this program

VeloxaCare is being developed under Veloxa Technology Limited to help clinics and pharmacies keep following up with patients after they leave the facility.

The product uses WhatsApp to support medication reminders, appointment reminders, patient check-ins, and early follow-up when a patient may be dropping out of care. The clinic receives a dashboard and useful summaries without creating additional work for the nurses or care team.

At this stage, the most important thing is not only to continue building the product. We need to put the product in front of real healthcare providers, understand their needs, secure pilot users, and build a clear path to paying customers.

This program is therefore focused on client acquisition, partnerships, pilot implementation, and learning from the market.

## 2. What I want us to achieve

By the end of August, I want us to have:

1. A clear understanding of our best first customers.
2. A qualified list of clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies to pursue.
3. A consistent process for outreach, discovery meetings, demos, proposals, and follow-up.
4. At least one active VeloxaCare pilot.
5. A second pilot either signed or ready to launch.
6. Evidence that healthcare providers see value in the product.
7. A repeatable client-acquisition system that we can continue using in September and beyond.

Our first focus should remain narrow. We should prioritise private healthcare providers and start with practical use cases such as hypertension, diabetes, medication adherence, missed appointments, and patient retention.

## 5. Who we are targeting

### Primary targets

- Private clinics
- Specialist clinics
- Small and medium-sized hospitals
- Pharmacies serving chronic-care patients
- Healthcare groups with multiple branches

### Initial decision-makers

- Clinic owners
- Medical directors
- Hospital administrators
- Pharmacy owners
- Operations managers
- Practice managers
- Heads of nursing or patient-care teams

### The problems we want to understand

We should find out whether the organisation is experiencing:

- Patients forgetting or discontinuing their medication.
- Missed appointments and poor follow-up.
- Patients disappearing between visits.
- Nurses spending too much time on manual reminders.
- Poor visibility into patients who may be at risk.
- Loss of repeat visits, refills, or long-term patient relationships.
- Difficulty identifying patients who need attention before their next appointment.

We should not force the product onto every organisation. We should first confirm that the problem is real, important, and worth paying to solve.

## 6. Program workstreams

### Workstream 1: Market research and lead generation

We will create a focused list of potential customers and identify the right people to contact.

### Workstream 2: Sales conversations and discovery

We will use discovery meetings to understand the provider's current process, pain points, budget, decision-making process, and readiness to test VeloxaCare.

### Workstream 3: Product demonstrations

We will demonstrate VeloxaCare using examples that are relevant to the provider's specialty. The demo should show the business value, not only the technology.

### Workstream 4: Pilot acquisition and implementation

We will offer selected providers a structured pilot with clear objectives, responsibilities, timelines, and measures of success.

### Workstream 5: Client learning and product improvement

We will record what prospects and pilot users say, identify repeated objections, and use the information to improve the product, pitch, pricing, and onboarding process.

## 9. Sales pipeline structure

Every prospect should be placed in one of the following stages:

1. New prospect identified.
2. Contacted.
3. Decision-maker reached.
4. Discovery meeting booked.
5. Discovery completed.
6. Demo completed.
7. Proposal sent.
8. Pilot negotiation.
9. Pilot signed.
10. Pilot active.
11. Paying customer.

Every record must include an owner, the last action taken, the next action, and the next follow-up date.

## 10. Pilot structure

The pilot should be presented as a structured business test, not as an informal free service.

Each pilot should define:

- The name of the clinic or pharmacy.
- The business problem being addressed.
- The patient group or condition being followed up.
- The pilot start and end dates.
- The number of patients involved.
- The responsibilities of the provider's team.
- The responsibilities of the VeloxaCare team.
- The information that will be measured.
- The review dates.
- The expected next step after the pilot.

Possible measures include:

- Patient response rate.
- Medication follow-up completion.
- Missed appointment recovery.
- Number of patients flagged for staff attention.
- Staff time saved.
- Patient satisfaction.
- Repeat visits or refills where the data is available.
